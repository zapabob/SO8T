## Key Technologies
- **SO8T Quadrality Reasoning**: Structured thinking with `<think-task>`, `<think-analysis>`, `<think-safety>`, and `<think-policy>`.
- **DeepSeek-style GRPO**: Group Relative Policy Optimization for enhanced mathematical and OSINT extraction reasoning.
- **mHC Manifold Integration**: Manifold Harmonic Correction to stabilize weights during LoRA/QLoRA adaptation.
- **Unsloth & imatrix**: Ultra-fast training and high-precision quantization for local inference (RTX 3060 optimized).
